### Gurney's Seed (general guide)
Source: Gurney's planting guide. Read 2026-11-18.
Status: **verified for the general rules only**. No crop-by-crop or zone-specific dates on the page.
- Start seeds about eight weeks before it is safe to transplant outdoors.
- Peppers: about 90 days indoors, per the page.
- Root crops are not started indoors.

### Johnny's Seeds: seed planting schedule calculator
Source: https://www.johnnyseeds.com/growers-library/seed-planting-schedule-calculator.html (linked from johnnyseeds.com). Read 2026-11-19 in two passes; the whole table was read through Zinnia.
Status: **verified as read**, with caveats below.

How the table works, as read:
- Each row gives weeks to start, a start-indoors date range, a set-out rule ("X weeks before", "On frost-free date", "X weeks after"), and a set-out date range.
- The page's dates are computed from a single reference frost-free date of **13 September**. The page does not label that date. It is inferred from the "On frost-free date" rows, which all show 13-Sep. The page says the dates are the same from year to year.
- The rule and week counts transfer to any location. Apply them to your own spring frost date, not to the calendar dates.
- Asterisks mark crops "usually direct-sown, but can be started indoors."
- Several rows have missing end dates (shown as "–" or "none" in the source). Those are gaps in the source, not zeros.

Correction: an earlier reply said broccoli is set out 1–2 weeks after frost. That was wrong. The table gives broccoli 2 weeks **before** frost-free date. Tomatoes at 1–2 weeks after is correct.
